# BuffPower

Buff management for Mages, Priests and Druids, inspired by PallyPower.

BuffPower shows your raid or party grouped the way the game groups it, with each group's buff status at a glance, and lets you buff straight from that overview. It works for 5-man dungeons as well as 40-man raids, and supports WoW Classic Era, Burning Crusade Classic and WoW Forever.

## Features

* **Raid overview**: A compact, group-based view of the whole raid. A group shows green when everyone has your buffs and red when someone is missing one.
* **Smart buffing**: Picks the group or single-target version of a buff based on your assignments and on which spells you have learned.
* **Manual overrides**: Want Thorns only on the tanks? Scroll the mouse wheel over a player to assign utility buffs to individuals.
* **Combat-safe**: Built on Blizzard's secure action buttons, so click-to-cast works in combat without "action blocked" errors.
* **Class filtering**: Choose which classes get which buff (for example, skip Intellect on Warriors and Rogues) from a simple options grid.

## Supported classes

* **Mage**: Arcane Intellect / Arcane Brilliance, Amplify Magic, Dampen Magic
* **Priest**: Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit, Shadow Protection
* **Druid**: Mark / Gift of the Wild, Thorns

## How to use

* **Left-click a group**: Casts the group-wide version of the assigned buff.
* **Right-click a group**: Casts the single-target buff on the next player in that group who needs it.
* **Mouse wheel over a player**: Cycles through manual utility assignments (for example, forcing Thorns on).
* **`/bp`**: Toggles the main window.
* **`/bp options`**: Opens the configuration.

## Compatibility

* WoW Classic Era (including Hardcore and Season of Discovery)
* Burning Crusade Classic
* WoW Forever (beta)

On WoW Forever, the game hides buffs from addons during combat. BuffPower keeps showing the status it saw before the fight, and buffs cast during combat show up once it ends.
